Delete your Sunkissed account

Sunkissed is operated by Livbrid. You can delete your account and the data stored with it from inside the app. You do not need to email us first.

Steps

  1. Open the Sunkissed app and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app, email hello@sunkissed.app from the address on your profile, or include the phone number on the account, and ask us to delete it.

What is deleted

Deleting the account removes:

  • Your sign-in and profile, including your name, email, phone number, date of birth, nationality, and country of residency.
  • Your travel preferences and notes.
  • Trips you created and your push notification token.

What is kept

A WhatsApp invite that was already delivered stays in that chat. We do not keep a separate copy of your account after deletion, and we do not hold the deleted data for an extra retention period.
