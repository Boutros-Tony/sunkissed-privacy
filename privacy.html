Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Sunkissed is a trip-planning app operated by Livbrid. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Sunkissed iOS and Android apps, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Contact: hello@sunkissed.app or +30 21 0304 2210.

Information you give us

  • Phone number, used to create your account and send a WhatsApp verification code.
  • First name, last name, and password.
  • Date of birth, nationality, country of residency, and email address.
  • Travel preferences, including style, activities, and whether you have been to Greece.
  • Trip details you enter, such as group, dates, nights, and notes.
  • Names and phone numbers of people you invite to a trip.

Information collected automatically

  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when someone invites you to a trip.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the app, such as app version and the fact that a request succeeded or failed.

We do not use the app to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

How we use it

  • To create and protect your account.
  • To plan and save your trips.
  • To send WhatsApp codes and trip invites.
  • To send push notifications you have allowed.
  • To answer support requests.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Supabase, which stores your account and trip data.
  • Meta (WhatsApp), which delivers verification codes and invite messages to phone numbers you provide.
  • Expo, which delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Apple or Google, when you download the app from their stores.

If you invite someone, we send that person your first name and the fact that you added them to a trip.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and trips while your account is open. If you delete your account in the app (Profile), we delete your account and the trip data tied to it. Invite messages already delivered to WhatsApp stay in those chats.

Your choices

  • You can turn notifications off in your phone settings.
  • You can edit profile details in the app.
  • You can delete your account from Profile.
  • You can email hello@sunkissed.app to ask what we hold or to request a correction.

Children

Sunkissed is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after that date means you accept the updated policy.
